Kitchen Box

Ran out of something? Need to remember something? Tell Alexa!

1Add one thing

“Alexa, ask Kitchen Box to add  .” Alexa says “Added …” and you're done.

2Add lots of things

You“Alexa, open Kitchen Box”
Alexa“What should I add?”
You“add milk”
“add bananas”
You, when done“no”

3Things you can say

  • add oat milk
  • we're out of dish soap
  • we need diapers
  • buy paper towels
  • remind me to book the dentist
  • add batteries for the toy digger

🛑 To finish

Say “no” or “stop”. If you just say one thing (step 1), Alexa finishes by herself.

💡 Tips

  • Say “ask” or “open”, not “tell”.
  • One thing at a time works best.
  • Speak clearly. Alexa writes exactly what you say.
For grown-ups: everything goes into the “Kitchen Inbox” Google Sheet. Ankita's assistant checks it every morning and adds things to the shopping baskets. Please don't use Alexa's own shopping list for these. Plain “Alexa, add milk” (without “Kitchen Box”) goes there instead.
